Sources & method

Each chart uses the Hilton disclosure listed below. Fiscal years ended December 31; figures are reported values unless noted.

  • Honors membership: Hilton 10-K, “Business Overview,” FY2022 10-K · FY2023 10-K · FY2024 10-K · FY2025 10-K.
  • Honors share of system occupancy: Hilton 2025 and 2026 proxy statements, company performance summaries; GWCCA board presentation, Sept. 25, 2025, p. 64. 2025 proxy · 2026 proxy · GWCCA presentation.
  • System-wide RevPAR: Hilton annual reports, FY2022 10-K · FY2023 10-K · FY2024 10-K · FY2025 10-K. The FY2023 +12.6% recovery is in FY2023 10-K, p. 50; FY2025 deceleration context is in FY2025 10-K, p. 44.
  • Net unit growth: Hilton annual reports, FY2022 10-K · FY2023 10-K · FY2024 10-K · FY2025 10-K. The 2024 additions and partner-room figures are in FY2024 10-K, p. 4.
  • Development pipeline rooms: Hilton annual reports, FY2022 10-K · FY2023 10-K · FY2024 10-K · FY2025 10-K.
  • Adjusted EBITDA: Hilton annual reports, FY2022 10-K · FY2023 10-K · FY2024 10-K · FY2025 10-K; reported USD millions converted to USD billions for display.

Inflection points: FY2023 RevPAR recovery, FY2024 net unit growth step-up, and FY2025 RevPAR slowdown are annotated on their charts with the period, change, and cited cause.

Membership and occupancy share are rounded as reported. No estimates are introduced.
