Our company & assignment

Hilton Worldwide Holdings is a global hotel company with an asset-light model, a large loyalty program, and a growing pipeline of managed and franchised hotels. Its transformation story includes using digital services and Hilton Honors to strengthen the guest relationship while expanding the network.

For this assignment, we used six public KPIs from Hilton’s disclosures to look for signs of value creation. Together they track loyalty reach, member stays, hotel performance, network growth, future room supply, and earnings.

Our question: Is Hilton turning stronger loyalty and a larger hotel network into durable operating and financial value?
